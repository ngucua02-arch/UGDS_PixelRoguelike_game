# Game Vision

- Project: Neon Roguelike Prototype (working title)
- One-sentence Pitch: A single-player top-down pixel-art roguelike where room combat and randomized items create a distinct build on each run.
- Target Player: TBD
- Core Fantasy: TBD

## Game Pillars

1. **Readable, responsive combat** — movement, dodging, attacks, damage, and danger are immediately understandable in a compact top-down arena.
2. **Build-changing item choices** — rewards change how the player's weapon or combat decisions work, rather than only adding small numerical bonuses.
3. **Fast repeatable runs** — a short sequence of rooms reaches a clear win/loss outcome and makes restarting easy.
4. **Pixel-art neon identity** — high-contrast silhouettes and restrained neon accents keep combat readable while establishing the game's visual direction.

## Design Check

Prototype features should strengthen at least one pillar. Defer features that do not help test the core combat, item choices, replay loop, or visual readability.

## Non-goals / Boundaries

- No permanent meta-progression in the first playable prototype.
- No large content library, branching story, multiplayer, online services, or complex procedural generation.
- Temporary visuals and audio are acceptable during core-loop validation.
